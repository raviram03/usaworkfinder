About USAWorkFinder

USAWorkFinder is a free job search tool built to help people find real, current job openings across the United States in one simple place. Instead of jumping between dozens of company career pages, you can search by title, location, pay range, job type, and how recently a role was posted — all in a few clicks.

We aggregate live job listings from across the U.S. labor market, covering industries from accounting and finance to customer service, retail, healthcare, and technology. Our goal is simple: make the job search process faster and less overwhelming, especially for people re-entering the workforce, switching careers, or applying from outside the U.S. for remote roles.

USAWorkFinder is an independent project, not affiliated with any single employer or job board. We're actively improving the site — adding better filters, guides, and tools to make your job search easier.

Have feedback or a suggestion? Visit our Contact page — we'd love to hear from you.
