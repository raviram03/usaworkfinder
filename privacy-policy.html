Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how USAWorkFinder ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit usaworkfinder.netlify.app (the "Site").

Our Site may use cookies set by Google Analytics and, in the future, by advertising partners such as Google AdSense, to measure traffic and display relevant ads. You can disable cookies through your browser settings at any time.

Job listings displayed on this Site are sourced from third-party job data providers. Clicking a job listing will take you to the employer's or job board's own website, which has its own privacy practices. We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of external sites.

Advertising

If we display ads through Google AdSense, Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. You can learn more and opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect any information collected through this Site, though no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

Children's Privacy

This Site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy
